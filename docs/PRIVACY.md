# Privacy design status

OutcomeOS requires explicit processing purpose and separate advertising permission on canonical events. Tenant-scoped retention, export, erasure/pseudonymisation, legal-retention exceptions, PII masking and deletion audit records are planned but not yet implemented. Accounting facts will be retained or pseudonymised rather than destructively edited where law or reconciliation requires them.

This repository does not claim GDPR compliance, certification or completion of organisational/legal controls.
